FAQ

Good to know.

Short answers. More in the README.

Do I need a MacBook with a notch?

It's built around the notch, but on a Mac without one it appears as a small floating pill at the top of the screen. Hovering opens it the same way.

Does my code leave my machine?

Agent events stay on your Mac by default, using a local socket. The app has no built-in analytics. Optional phone notifications and SSH use network services when you configure them. Your agents may use cloud services independently.

Will it change my agent settings?

You review integration changes before connecting agents. Vibe Notch backs up configurations and removes its managed entries when you disconnect.

Which agents are supported?

Claude Code and Codex have the deepest support: approvals, questions, diffs and history. Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Cursor and the Claude-style forks connect through their hook systems.

Is it free?

Yes — open source under GPL-3.0.
